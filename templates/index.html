AI Soil Nutrient Diagnostic System

📖 About Raphael

Raphael is an AI soil diagnostic system utilizing a Multi-Head Neural Network (mhNN) to classify macronutrients into DEFICIENT, SUFFICIENT, or HIGH.

🎯 Model Learned Thresholds

Trained on European topsoil benchmark data:

Nutrient Deficient Sufficient High
N (g/kg) < 1.93 1.93 – 3.02 > 3.02
P (mg/kg) < 14.8 14.8 – 29.7 > 29.7
K (mg/kg) < 99.7 99.7 – 198.6 > 198.6

🧪 Chemical Constraint Layer (CCL)

Rule-based constraint layer evaluating pH-related availability restrictions independently from neural network predictions:

  • Nitrogen: restricted if pH < 6.0 or pH > 8.0
  • Phosphorus: restricted if pH < 6.0 or pH > 7.5
  • Potassium: restricted if pH < 6.0 or pH > 9.0
